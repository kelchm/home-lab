## Upstream direction and contribution order

The September 6 issue review found seven open issues, all authored by the repository owner, with no comments, assignees or milestones. They establish desired outcomes, not a committed delivery schedule. The merged [canonical workflow PR 603](https://github.com/CarstVaartjes/vonk-forge/pull/603) is stronger evidence of implemented direction: canonical contracts, shared UI/CLI actions, separate model/image preparation, NAS caching and exact provenance. Open status does not imply that none of an issue's requested behavior exists.

| Upstream request | Implication for this evaluation and our fixes |
| --- | --- |
| [593: verified NAS cache and LAN distribution](https://github.com/CarstVaartjes/vonk-forge/issues/593) | Major cache/distribution work is already in PR 603. Measure its actual current path and interruption behavior before proposing a parallel cache or applying old transfer conclusions. Our 27.65 MB/s image upload remains a historical measurement of the deployed generation. |
| [594: common operation progress](https://github.com/CarstVaartjes/vonk-forge/issues/594) | Progress should continue independently of ordinary telemetry. Our runtime-observation scheduler fixes freshness starvation; it does not deliver the full progress, throughput, ETA or stall contract. |
| [595: deployment provenance and evidence](https://github.com/CarstVaartjes/vonk-forge/issues/595) | Distinguish merged, deployed, installed, loaded and physically accepted states. Fleet's corrected completeness projection is a small compatible fix, not the requested evidence UI. |
| [596: preflight in the actual service sandbox](https://github.com/CarstVaartjes/vonk-forge/issues/596) | Probe parsing and fresh host-inventory admission fit the intended direction. Keep real service identity, namespace and storage restrictions when investigating native builds. |
| [597: durable lifecycle phases and recovery](https://github.com/CarstVaartjes/vonk-forge/issues/597) | Heartbeat, route and retained-inspection fixes address narrow failure cases. General checkpoint/resume and per-node recovery remain broader work. |
| [598: bounded sanitized failure evidence](https://github.com/CarstVaartjes/vonk-forge/issues/598) | The classifier extends existing [sanitized build diagnostics](https://github.com/CarstVaartjes/vonk-forge/pull/556); it does not implement evidence bundles or justify raw log exposure. |
| [551: updater bootstrap recovery](https://github.com/CarstVaartjes/vonk-forge/issues/551) | Trusted ARM64 package activation, rollback and physical canary acceptance remain separate from a signed source commit or passing workspace tests. |

My recommended upstream order is architecture mapping and retained-inspection safety first because they are small current-main regressions affecting ordinary execution, then probe parsing, Fleet completeness, egress admission and the diagnostic classifier as independent corrections. Follow with heartbeat and the coherent route-recovery change. Submit the observation scheduler last, with its controller dependency and explicit concurrency/failure evidence. The two old UI patches should be omitted. None of these small contributions should claim to close a broad roadmap issue.

The fresh-inventory egress gate follows upstream's [live-inventory admission work](https://github.com/CarstVaartjes/vonk-forge/pull/589). Routes and observations preserve [per-rank liveness authority](https://github.com/CarstVaartjes/vonk-forge/pull/477) and [receipt-key binding](https://github.com/CarstVaartjes/vonk-forge/pull/599). These relationships support contributing targeted current-contract fixes rather than reviving retired UI or schema behavior. The larger unfinished egress-service cleanup attempt and speculative transfer optimizations remain held.
